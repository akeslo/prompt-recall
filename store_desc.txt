Prompt Recall: Your AI Prompt Library

Stop retyping the same prompts. Prompt Recall lets you save, search, and instantly copy your best AI prompts — right from your browser toolbar.

KEY FEATURES

Save & Organise
Capture any prompt with a title and tags. Pin favourites to the top. Three view modes (compact, list, full) adapt to how you work.

Instant Search & Tag Filtering
Full-text search across titles, content, and tags. Click any tag pill to filter instantly. Sort by most recent, most used, last used, alphabetical, or by tag.

Template Variables
Write prompts with {{placeholders}} and Prompt Recall automatically prompts you to fill them in before copying — turning your prompts into reusable templates.

Prompt Variants
Store alternative phrasings under a single prompt. Switch between Primary and named variants with one click — perfect for A/B-testing tone or style.

Markdown & Syntax Highlighting
Prompts render with full Markdown formatting and syntax-highlighted code blocks, so long structured prompts stay readable at a glance.

Before / After Examples
Attach reference images to a prompt to document what it produces — great for image-generation workflows.

PIN Lock
Protect sensitive prompts with a global PIN. Locked prompts hide their content until unlocked, without removing them from your library.

Import / Export
Back up your entire library as JSON or migrate it to another device in seconds. Load 10 built-in sample prompts covering coding, writing, research, and image generation to hit the ground running.

Resizable Panel
Drag the resize handle to set the panel height that fits your screen and workflow.

WHO IT'S FOR
Prompt Recall is built for anyone who works with AI models daily — developers, writers, researchers, designers, and power users who are tired of hunting through chat history to find that one prompt that worked perfectly.
